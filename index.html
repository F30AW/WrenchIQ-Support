Account & privacy support

Request account deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your WrenchIQ account and the personal data associated with it, even if you've uninstalled the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email wrenchiqapp@gmail.com, ideally from the email address registered to your WrenchIQ account.
  2. Use the subject WrenchIQ Account Deletion Request and tell us which WrenchIQ account you want deleted.
  3. We may need to verify that you own the account before making permanent changes.
  4. WrenchIQ Support will confirm your request, explain any applicable retention requirements and advise when deletion is complete.
Email account deletion request

If the button doesn't open your email app, send a message manually to wrenchiqapp@gmail.com. Never send us your password.

What happens to your data?

Once a request has been verified, WrenchIQ will arrange deletion of the account and associated personal data, including data such as:

  • Account and profile details
  • Saved Garage vehicles and associated maintenance records
  • Vehicle photos and other personally uploaded media
  • Personal social content, messages and connection records associated with the account, subject to applicable legal or safety retention obligations

Some information may need to be retained temporarily when required for legal compliance, security or fraud prevention. If this applies, we will explain the data involved, the reason and the applicable retention period when handling your request.

This is an account-deletion request, not merely account deactivation. Deletion may be irreversible, so save any information or photographs you want to keep before requesting it.

Need help with WrenchIQ?

For technical support, feedback or a question about your account, contact wrenchiqapp@gmail.com.
