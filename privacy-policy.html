Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WrenchIQ handles information when you use its vehicle maintenance, diagnosis and car-community features.

1. Who to contact

WrenchIQ is operated by the WrenchIQ app developer. For questions about your information or requests to access, correct or delete it, email wrenchiqapp@gmail.com.

2. Information collected and stored

  • Account and profile details: account ID, email address, username, display name, and optional profile details such as biography, county or general area, and profile picture.
  • Garage information: vehicles you add, vehicle specifications and identifying details such as registration number or VIN when provided, photos, modifications, maintenance entries, costs, mileage and notes you choose to save.
  • Community and communications: social posts, comments, uploaded pictures, private messages, friend and connection information, likes and other interactions, and reports or blocks made through community features.
  • Technical and security information: information needed to authenticate accounts, deliver cloud features, protect the service and investigate technical problems. Service and platform providers may process network or access information, such as IP addresses and request logs, in delivering those features.

Some information, such as an email address and username, is necessary to create an account. Uploading vehicle photographs, sharing a county, using Socials and adding most other profile details are optional.

3. Why we use information

  • To register accounts and support sign-in, authentication and account management.
  • To display and synchronise a user's Garage and vehicle maintenance information.
  • To provide community profiles, posts, replies, messages and connections.
  • To operate safety features such as blocking, reporting, troubleshooting and moderation.
  • To respond to user support and privacy requests, maintain reliability and protect user accounts.

We process information as needed to provide requested app features and for legitimate service and security purposes, or with consent when the law requires it.

4. Visibility and sharing

Content posted to a public community feed can be seen by other users. Profile details, shared vehicle photos and similar material may also be visible according to the applicable feature or privacy setting. Private messages are intended for their participants, subject to necessary service and safety administration.

Consider carefully what you make public. Other people may save or capture information you have published.

5. Services used to operate WrenchIQ

WrenchIQ uses Supabase to support account authentication, database storage and media uploads. Some app information may also be stored locally on your device. These services process information to provide WrenchIQ features.

Google Play and Android services may separately process installation or platform-related information under their own applicable privacy terms. This public support website is hosted through GitHub Pages, which may process site access data under GitHub's privacy terms.

Data processing locations and the service providers used may change as the app develops. This policy will be updated when there are material changes.

6. Security

App-to-cloud communications use encrypted HTTPS connections. WrenchIQ implements account-based access controls designed to keep private records separated between users. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Never send a password to WrenchIQ Support.

7. How long information is kept

We generally retain account information and related content while an account is active and as needed to operate the services the user has chosen to use. When content is removed or an account-deletion request is verified, we arrange deletion of relevant records, subject to legitimate legal, security or dispute-resolution retention requirements.

If anything must be retained following a deletion request, WrenchIQ Support will explain the information concerned and the applicable reason or period. Backup copies or service logs may take additional time to expire as part of normal technical retention processes.

8. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose whether to supply optional profile details, photos and social content. Some items can be edited or removed through WrenchIQ. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, email wrenchiqapp@gmail.com. We may ask you to verify account ownership.

Depending on applicable UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, and to data portability where applicable. You may raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Age requirements

WrenchIQ is currently intended for users aged 18 and over. It is not designed for children to create accounts or participate in its community.

10. Updates and questions

We may revise this policy when WrenchIQ's features, service providers or data practices change. This page will show the most recent update date.

For privacy enquiries: wrenchiqapp@gmail.com.
